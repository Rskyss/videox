Frequently Asked Questions

Is VideoX free to use?

Yes. VideoX is completely free. No registration, no software installation and no payment is required.

Do downloaded videos have a watermark?

No. Videos parsed from Douyin, TikTok, Bilibili and Xiaohongshu are saved without watermarks and keep their original resolution.

Which platforms are supported?

Douyin, Bilibili, Xiaohongshu, YouTube, TikTok and Twitter/X. YouTube and Bilibili support 360p, 720p and 1080p.

Why did my link fail to parse?

The link may be incorrect, or the video has been deleted, set to private or is still under review. Copy the share link again and retry.

Do you store the videos I download?

Downloads from every supported platform use temporary server storage. The file is deleted after transfer or expires automatically within one hour.
